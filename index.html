Curriculum Vitae

I am a clinical AI researcher focused on three intersecting problems: making medical AI safe and interpretable, building diagnostic tools for under-resourced settings, and closing the geographic fairness gap in biomedical machine learning.

My current work includes developing an uncertainty-aware multimodal transformer for early endometriosis detection, and auditing confidence calibration in clinical LLMs under geographic distribution shift — a paper currently under review at ACL.

About

I hold a BSc in Computer Science (CGPA 3.81/5.0, Top 5%, Ambrose Alli University) and am an independent researcher at the Machine Learning Collective (MLC) and a research collaborator at HuggingFace Science, where I co-led model development for longitudinal microbiome analysis. My translational work bridges computation and clinical practice through collaboration with the CMHS research group at ABUAD

I am also investigating representation bias in African clinical datasets, building infrastructure to evaluate how classical models and LLMs perform across populations they were never trained on. I am currently seeking a PhD position to deepen this work at scale.

I care deeply about teaching and community. I currently work as a Coding and Robotics Instructor for primary and secondary school students through EduPoint Ltd. and have taught AI and data structures at university level, because I believe every student deserves early, quality access to the kind of computational thinking that took me years to find.

I also enjoy exploring creative projects such as woodworking and innovative footwear design. Handcraft helps me think through problems differently and often informs how I approach technical and scientific challenges.
