03 · Proposed Architecture

A three-stage diagnostic pipeline

The system is designed to be deployable in settings where specialist imaging access is delayed or cost-prohibitive. Each stage is independently useful; together they form a full diagnostic pathway.

01

Symptom-text screening layer

A lightweight NLP classifier processing structured symptom and clinical history input as a first-stage triage tool, flagging at-risk patients before imaging is required. Particularly relevant in settings where imaging access is delayed or financially prohibitive.

02

Multimodal fusion module

An uncertainty-aware transformer integrating MRI and ultrasound imaging with structured clinical data. Architectural choices are informed by attention-based fusion literature. Uncertainty quantification is a core design requirement, not an afterthought.

03

Geographic generalization evaluation

A systematic evaluation framework testing diagnostic reliability across Western and African patient subpopulations. Draws on distribution shift methodology developed in the AfricaBench collaboration to surface performance gaps that aggregate metrics would otherwise obscure.

04 · Research Questions

What this project is trying to answer

RQ1

Do models trained on Western endometriosis imaging data maintain diagnostic reliability when applied to African clinical data?

RQ2

Can a symptom-text screening layer serve as a viable first-stage triage tool in settings where imaging access is delayed or cost-prohibitive?

RQ3

How does uncertainty quantification behave under geographic distribution shift, and can it reliably signal when a model is operating outside its training distribution?
